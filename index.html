Baby Heartbeat Listener

Please note: this is a listening toy, not a medical device. A phone microphone often can’t pick up a baby’s heartbeat, and silence does not mean anything is wrong. Never use it for reassurance. If you feel less movement than usual or have any worry, call your doctor or go to the hospital.

Tips
  • Use headphones. Without them the speaker feeds back into the mic and squeals.
  • Works best after about 20 weeks, lying back in a very quiet room.
  • Press the bottom edge of the phone (where the mic is) gently on bare skin, low on the belly, and move it slowly. Try a paper cone or cardboard tube for extra focus.
  • The fast “whoosh‑whoosh” is the baby (about 110–160/min). A slower thump (about 60–90/min) is you. The estimate is rough and can be wrong.
  • Stay still and hold your breath briefly if you can; breathing and rustling skin are loud.
